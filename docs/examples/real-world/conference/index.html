FAQ

Useful details before you book.

Who is Field Notes for?

It is made for product designers, engineers, researchers, writers, and leaders who shape digital products. Talks assume professional experience but not a specific discipline or seniority.

How accessible is the venue?

Theater Rotterdam has step-free access, accessible toilets, induction loops, and reserved wheelchair spaces. Live captioning is provided on the main stage. Tell us about other requirements in the registration form.

Can I transfer or cancel a ticket?

You can transfer a pass at no cost until 12 June. Cancellations made by 18 May receive a full refund; after that date, you may transfer the pass or receive access to session recordings.

Are travel and accommodation included?

No. The venue is a seven-minute walk from Rotterdam Centraal. Registered attendees receive partner hotel rates and a practical city guide.

Will sessions be recorded?

Main-stage talks will be recorded and shared with attendees. Studios and audience discussions remain off camera so participants can work openly.
