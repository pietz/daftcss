Our story

Rooted here, open to the season

Chef-owners Mara Chen and Eli Brooks opened Juniper & Ash in 2019 after a decade spent cooking across the Pacific Northwest. Their idea was simple: build a generous restaurant around close relationships with growers, fishers, and makers.

The hearth is the center of our kitchen. Alder and oak bring smoke, patience, and a little unpredictability to produce from the Willamette Valley, seafood from the Oregon coast, and meat raised on nearby pasture.

“The best meals feel both considered and completely at ease.”
— Mara Chen, chef and co-owner

Guest information

Before you arrive

Can you accommodate allergies and dietary needs?

Yes, with advance notice. We regularly accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free diets. Because our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, shellfish, and gluten, we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Is the restaurant accessible?

Our entrance, dining room, bar, and all-gender restroom are step-free. Tables can accommodate wheelchairs, and service animals are welcome. Add your needs to the reservation or call us so we can prepare.

What is the dress code?

There is none. Guests arrive in everything from hiking boots to date-night best. Wear what makes you comfortable.

May I bring children?

Absolutely. High chairs are available, and we can prepare a simple plate for younger diners. Please include children and infants in your party size.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel or change your table at least 24 hours ahead. Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged $25 per guest. If illness or travel disruption changes your plans, please call us.
