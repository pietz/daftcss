Common questions

Before we start

What makes a project a good fit?

There is a real decision to make, meaningful access to stakeholders or customers, and a sponsor willing to act on what we learn. I am most useful when the answer is not obvious and alignment matters.

Do you work with in-house design or strategy teams?

Often. I can add senior capacity, provide an outside point of view, or lead a focused workstream while leaving tools and capability with the internal team.

Can you join an existing project?

Yes, if there is a clear mandate and enough room to improve the outcome. A short diagnostic is usually the best way to understand where I can contribute.

Do you work outside Europe?

Yes. Research and workshops can be remote or in person. Travel is scoped separately, and I design every engagement to protect time with the people closest to the problem.

What decision is on your desk?

Share the context, even if the brief is not tidy yet. I reply personally within two working days.
