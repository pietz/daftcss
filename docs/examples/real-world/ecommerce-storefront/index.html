Field notes · Issue 08

Coffee with a clear provenance

Producer Catalina Herrera separates every day lot, then dries the coffee slowly on raised beds. We pay $5.20 per pound at origin, 2.6× the commodity price.

“Longer drying gives us more clarity in the cup and more control over our season.”

Good to know

Questions before you order?

Freshness, grind size, subscriptions, and returns.

When will my coffee be roasted?

We roast Monday through Thursday and ship within one business day. Every bag is marked with its roast date, never a best-before date.

Can you grind coffee for my brewer?

Yes. Choose whole bean, espresso, AeroPress, filter, or French press on the product page. For the freshest cup, we still recommend grinding just before brewing.

How do subscriptions work?

Choose one or two bags every two or four weeks. You can change the coffee, skip a shipment, or cancel before the next roast date.

What is your return policy?

Unused equipment can be returned within 30 days. If a coffee is not right for you, tell us what you tasted and we will help choose a replacement.
